Our Story

A hotel built around its verandah, not its lobby

The Verandah opened in 2010 in a renovated courtyard house. We kept the arches, the tiled floors, and the idea that a hotel should feel like a long, unhurried visit to a friend's home — not a transaction.

What makes us different

  • 🏡
    18 rooms, no more

    We stayed small on purpose, so every guest is known by name by their second morning.

  • 🍲
    A kitchen, not a buffet

    Every dish on our menu is cooked to order — see our full restaurant menu for what's on today.

  • 🌿
    Courtyard-first design

    Rooms wrap around a shared garden courtyard instead of facing a car park or corridor.

  • 🤝
    Local, always

    Staff, produce, and craftsmanship are sourced from within the city wherever possible.
